Tree shrew

Tree shrew

A single-cell atlas of tree shrew organogenesis, mapping cell-type-specific candidate cis-regulatory elements across three developmental stages.

Overview

About this project

TOPA profiles 3,656,332 nuclei from six whole embryos at E23.5, E25.5 and E27.5. It resolves 24 major cell classes and 197 cell types, and defines 1,238,436 candidate cis-regulatory elements.
